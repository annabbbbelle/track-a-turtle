About the Project

Sea turtles migrate thousands of miles across open ocean every year. By applying predictive AI modeling to historical movement patterns and satellite oceanographic variables, this project aims to forecast sea turtle movement. This helps marine researchers anticipate potential interactions with commercial fisheries and changing climate corridors.

How It Works: Telemetry & AI Classification Pipeline

1

GPS Telemetry Ingestion

Each turtle records daily GPS fixes ({date, lat, lon}) spanning 150 consecutive days across ocean basins.

2

Spherical Kinematic Metrics Calculation

Our JavaScript engine (analyze.js) applies the Haversine Formula to compute real great-circle distance between coordinates, compass course bearings, daily cruising speed, speed variance/consistency, and average turning angles.

3

Rule-Based Dynamic AI Personality Classification

Instead of hard-coding character traits, our classifier evaluates key decision-tree thresholds (displacement ratio, speed consistency percentage, and turning angle variance) to assign each turtle its archetype in real-time!
